Got questions?

Here are the most common questions about Flex Academy.

Is this for complete beginners?

Yes. Launch is built for people starting from zero, with a clear path to your first unit.

What if I already operate STR units?

Scale is designed for operators who want to turn a few units into a real operating company.

Do I need to own property?

No. The playbook covers several ways to start, including renting and partnering.

What's included in Launch?

The launch framework, property strategy, templates, live group sessions and the first-unit playbook.

What's included in Scale?

A 12-week programme, weekly group sessions, founder 1:1, operator community, SOP library and Base360 access.

How much does it cost?

The webinar is free. Programme pricing is shared on the webinar and the strategy call.

Is Base360 included?

Base360 access is part of the Scale programme.
